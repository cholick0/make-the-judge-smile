One line, 200 characters at most, about a small good thing that happened today — an entry for the [$5 "Make the Judge Smile" bounty on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-make-the-judge-smile-9228676d).

> My neighbour's cat, who has ignored me for two years, walked in this morning, sat on my laptop, and purred like we'd always been friends.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and self-checks the 1–200 character, single-line gate before writing.
